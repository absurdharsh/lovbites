The LovBites Story

Some Places Serve Food.
We Create Memories.

Nestled in the heart of Dhanbad at Om Sai Plaza, LovBites Café & Kitchen was born with a simple, heartfelt dream: to give our town a vibrant, warm haven where flavors tell stories and every customer feels cherished.

Kitchen of Honest Flavors

Every sauce is simmered from scratch, every biryani pot is sealed with care, and our recipes use only farm-fresh ingredients.

Celebrate Passions & Milestones

From first dates and anniversary surprises to crazy birthday cake bashes and college reunions — we set the perfect stage.

8 AM Morning Comfort to Late Night Cravings

Start your day with velvety cappuccinos and crispy bites, or wind down with hearty dinners under twinkling cafe lights.
